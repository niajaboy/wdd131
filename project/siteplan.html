RecycleNG - Website Planning Document

WDD 131 | Dynamic Web Fundamentals

1. Site Name

RecycleNG is short, easy to remember, and connects recycling with Nigeria.

2. Site Purpose

The purpose is to teach people in Nigeria about recycling and proper waste management. It explains recyclable materials and gives simple tips for sorting, reducing, and reusing waste.

3. Scenarios

  • How do I properly sort plastic bottles and bags?
  • What paper, glass, or metal items can I recycle at home?
  • Where can I ask a question or share my recycling experience?
