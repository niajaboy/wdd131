Practical ideas

Simple ways to manage waste better

You do not have to change everything at once. Start with one useful habit and build from there.

01

Use a separate recycling container

Keep a container or bag for clean recyclable materials. A simple separation system can make sorting easier at home.

02

Reduce single-use items

When possible, choose reusable bottles, bags, containers, and other items instead of disposable products.

03

Keep recyclables clean

Empty containers and remove leftover food when practical. Clean materials are easier to store and handle.

04

Do not burn household waste

Look for a responsible waste collection or disposal option available in your area instead.

05

Teach children early

Make sorting simple and fun. Children can learn to identify paper, plastic, glass, and metal.

06

Share good habits

Talk with neighbours, classmates, family members, or community groups about reducing waste.
